About us

A team that treats every build like a mission.

Apex Innovators is the student developer team of Parul Institute of Engineering and Technology (PIET), Vadodara. We are the people who raise hands when a hackathon is announced — and stay to turn the demo into a real project.

Who we are

Students first, builders always

We are undergraduate students — developers, designers and dreamers across the engineering disciplines at PIET. What binds us is simple: we would rather build than wait for someone else to.

Some of us fell in love with Java in the classroom, some with the terminal in a hostel room at 2 am, and all of us with the moment a hackathon demo finally works in front of a judging panel.

This platform is our shared memory and storefront at once: the projects we have shipped, the hackathons we have survived, the achievements we have earned, and the community we keep growing on campus.

Our north star

“Learn by building. Build by competing. Compete as a team — and write everything down so the next batch stands on our shoulders.”

Where we are

Parul Institute of Engineering and Technology, Vadodara, Gujarat, India — online and on campus.

What we value

The principles that keep us shipping

Build before perfect

A working demo beats a perfect plan. We timebox, prototype and iterate — polish follows proof.

Share every lesson

What one member learns becomes a post, a talk or a resource — knowledge compounds when it is shared.

Ship with integrity

Clean code, honest attribution and documentation we would want to read ourselves — always.

Compete to learn

Hackathons are our crash courses. We enter to learn in public, not just to win — though winning is nice too.

Open by default

Source, write-ups and verification links live in the open — anyone on campus can follow the breadcrumbs.

Lift the campus

Everything we archive is fuel for the students who come after us — a library of what is possible.

How we work

From announcement to archive

A repeatable loop that turns chaos into portfolio.

STEP 1

Scout & recruit

We watch hackathon calendars, pick the events that fit our skills, and pull together a balanced team — backend, frontend and ideas.

STEP 2

Scope & sprint

One clear pitch, one thin vertical slice, constant commits. Features get cut ruthlessly when the clock says so.

STEP 3

Demo & document

We present, then we debrief: what worked, what broke, what we would never do again — all captured on this platform.
